Artistic Vision

People often try to separate me into clean boxes labeling me a photographer, a video editor, or a designer. But those are just execution tools. At my core, I am an artist. Whether manipulating framing in a lens, timing a cinematic sequence in post-production, or composing clean interface layouts, my ultimate goal is translating raw atmosphere and concept into balanced structural experiences.
